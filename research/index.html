IM8

subscription, per-month pricepage
  • Stars + review count above the title. Trust strip (90-day money back, free shipping, NSF) under the nav.
  • "SUBSCRIBE & SAVE:" label, then two radio cards. Selected card expands: €91 /mo, "Billed €273 every 12 weeks", €3.03 / serving, 5 checks incl. "Cancel or pause anytime".
  • One-time is a plain underlined link under the cards: "One Time Purchase €130".
  • Sticky bar on scroll: plan dropdown + "ADD TO CART – €91 EUR/MO".
Take: the renewal cadence lives inside the plan card, next to the number, not in a footnote.

AG1

subscription vs one-time radiopage
  • Price line: €87.00 ~~€107.00~~ "Save 40% in Value", one sentence, then two option groups: Select Quantity, Choose Purchase Type.
  • Subscribe & Save selected by default; One Time Purchase €107 is the dearer radio.
  • "What's included with AG1 Subscription" box, then Buy Now, then "30-Day Money Back Guarantee • Secure Checkout".
  • Renewal only in the footnote: "Subscription continues at €87/month thereafter unless cancelled". "93% of customers choose Subscribe & Save" nudge.
Take: the word "subscription" is on the page three times above the button. Nobody can miss what they are buying.

Ritual

subscription default, one-time as linkpage
  • Title, 4 benefit checks, $26.40 ~~$33.00~~, Add to Cart, "30-day money-back guarantee. Free shipping." in one line under it.
  • "When You Subscribe, You Join the Club" card. One-time is "NON-MEMBER PRICING: Buy once — $37.50 + $10.99 Shipping" link.
Take: shortest buy box of the set. Guarantee + shipping as one quiet line under the button.

Storyworth

direct competitor, pay once a yearplans
  • Every button says "BUY NOW — STARTING AT $69". Trust row under the hero: Trustpilot 50k, NYT gift guide, press logos.
  • Plan page: three tier cards $59 / $99 / $199, each "1 book, 1 guided memoir, a year of weekly questions". "GIVE MORE" tag on the top tier.
  • Renewal in one small italic line under the top card only: "$99 annual renewal · cancel anytime".
  • "For me / As a gift" are tabs in How it works, not in the buy box.
Take: our buyer compares us to this. One number, a year, a book. Our "6 months then $39 a month" has to be at least this plain.

Remento

direct competitor, one pricepage
  • Hero: headline, 3 lines, Buy now + How it works, 4 checks, Trustpilot. No price on the homepage.
  • Checkout is a 6-step wizard (Storyteller, Delivery, Details, Extras, Add-ons, Review) with a cart on the right: €85, "1 hardcover book, 1 year of unlimited stories" and a "Your book includes" checklist.
Take: price and what is included sit side by side, in words a 70 year old reads once.

Spotminders

one-time e-commercepage
  • Trustpilot line, title, use-case chips. Numbered steps: "1. Select Quantity", "2. Select Colors".
  • Quantity rows: 1 Card $39 / Buy 2 Get +1 FREE $78 ~~$117~~ / Buy 3 Get +2 FREE $117 ~~$195~~, perks listed inside each row. Progress slider "Add 1 more to unlock 1 free card".
  • Price inside the button: "Add to Cart $39.00". Under it: "Almost sold out", "FREE shipping over $50", three trust pills, then the accordion.
  • FAQ answers the money question in words: "Are there any monthly fees? No monthly fees or subscriptions!"
Take: numbered steps, price in the button, the subscription question answered out loud.

NordVPN

term paid up front, then renews (closest to our model)page
  • Per-month number big: €4.49 /mo, Save 75%. Then, small, in the same card: "~~€493.83~~ €121.23 for the first 27 months … Renews at €219.48/year".
  • "30-day money-back guarantee" is the first check in every card.
Take: this is how the industry shows "pay for a term now, renews later": the total for the term and the renewal price in the plan card itself.
